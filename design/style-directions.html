How the colours share the screen

60% Lumen ground 30% ink and Fathom teal Dawn: actions Glow: evidence Flare: your voice

Each accent has one job, so they never compete. Teal and coral sit almost opposite on the colour wheel, so they never touch the same element. Dawn lilac is a pale tint, so it only fills controls, always with dark text.

What all five keep from the reference

  • Labels that say something true: a timestamp, a man-page section or a margin note, never decoration.
  • A reading voice for your words and the mentor's, separate from the interface.
  • Hard edges, one accent, and a second theme designed rather than inverted.
  • A figure that explains the project and that you can break: switch the buffer to 4 KB and watch the read count change.
